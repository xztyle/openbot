### Security

- On a Linux server, each Claude agent's process now runs in a jail of its own folders (Landlock). The agent, its shell and every MCP server and program it starts can reach its workspace, its space's shared folder, its own keys and the shared ones, the browser downloads, the toolchains of the home folder, and the Claude sign-in. They can no longer read another agent's keys or workspace, another space, the database, the keyring or the encrypted stores, or the environment of OpenBot's own processes in `/proc`, and they cannot signal those processes. Each agent gets its own home folder of links and its own temporary folder. `OPENBOT_AGENT_JAIL=off` turns the jail off, and `OPENBOT_AGENT_JAIL_STRICT=1` stops an agent that cannot be jailed instead of starting it without the jail. Codex and the other providers share one process between agents, so they are not jailed yet.
