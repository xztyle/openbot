### Fixed

- A Motion ticket that waits on you opens its agent's chat, not Motion: approve, decline or reply right there. The answer reaches the agent as your message and goes on the ticket as your comment.
- A comment on a Motion ticket now wakes its agents, mention or not. Your comment counts as your instruction; anyone else's is information only.
- The phone's Today badge counts the same items as the Today list.
- A push of a review branch is no longer stopped as a deployment. A blocked action now shows what was stopped, what the agent had read and why, with "Allow this once".
- What the agents learn about their memory applies by itself and shows in the inbox, where you keep it or revert it. It is no longer a Needs you item.
