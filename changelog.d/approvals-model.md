### Changed

- Approvals go to the asking agent's manager in the org chart, not to you: an OK on a ticket, an escalated review, a plan, a first mail to a new person, a publish or a lesson. Only money, deletions, credentials, legal commitments and security blocks reach you. A manager that does not answer in 2 hours passes the request up; after the top agent it comes to you.
- The inbox has a "Decided by agents" list, with Reopen for a ticket you want to decide yourself. The Trust page shows how many decisions you took this week and the share your agents took.
- What reaches you is in plain words: what is asked, why it matters, and what happens on a yes or a no. The technical text stays behind "Technical details".
