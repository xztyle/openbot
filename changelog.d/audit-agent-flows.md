### Changed

- An agent's routines and event checks now look like the rest of its settings: each list is one card of rows, and the editor puts its fields in titled groups of rows. A routine and a check still save only when you press Save.
- Deleting a routine or an event check now asks first in a confirmation, instead of swapping the buttons in place.
- The Pause and Resume buttons in the routine list are real buttons, not loose links.
- The Backup models page of an agent uses the same rows as its other settings, with a switch that saves at once.
- The New agent dialog names the face choice ("Look"), shows the model as a field like the name, and lets the suggestions wrap on a phone instead of scrolling out of view.

### Fixed

- In Schedule, an agent's face in a day and in the list of next runs showed as an empty pink square. It shows the face again.
- The routine editor's hint about quiet runs no longer shows a code; it says in plain words what to write in the instruction.
