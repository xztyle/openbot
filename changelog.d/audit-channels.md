### Changed

- Channel settings use the same layout as the other settings: sections with a title and a short explanation, then cards of labelled rows. This covers the channel details (About and Settings tabs) and the channel settings side panel.
- The channel topic, description, name, title, instructions and each agent's focus now save with one Save bar, with Reset, instead of saving when you leave the field. Switches and choices still save at once.
- Leave channel and Delete channel are real buttons in their own section. Delete still asks first.
- The Edit space and New space dialogs explain each field (name, emoji, color), and the color and emoji buttons are big enough to tap on a phone.
- The Add people and agents dialog and the Edit topic dialog show a visible label and a hint for their field.
