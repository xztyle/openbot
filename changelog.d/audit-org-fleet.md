### Changed

- The org chart's agent panel now looks like the other settings panels: groups for Now, Reports to, Direct reports and Actions, with rows that open the manager and each report. Change manager opens a picker window, and the choice saves at once with Undo. Putting the team to sleep is now a switch.
- Fleet cards show Message and Settings as named buttons, with the same spacing and text sizes as the rest of the app. In the list view the Motion ticket sits under the agent's status, and the buttons stay on the same line.
- On a phone, the Fleet card buttons and the Motion ticket on a card are big enough to tap.
