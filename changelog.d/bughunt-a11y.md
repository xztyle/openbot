### Fixed

- Keyboard and screen reader: closing a dialog, the shortcuts sheet or a thread with Escape gives
  the focus back to the button that opened it, also when a menu item opened it. Before, the focus
  fell to the top of the page.
- The shortcuts sheet (Ctrl+/ or ⌘/) takes the focus when it opens, so Tab and a screen reader stay
  in it.
- After Approve, Decline or an answer on Today or Money, the next decision takes the focus. After
  you delete a message, the message next to it takes the focus; after you leave a channel from its
  menu, the focus stays in the sidebar.
- Jump to (⌘K or Ctrl+K) tells a screen reader which row the arrow keys highlight.
- A screen reader reads a new message from someone else in the open channel or thread, and an
  agent's answer in your chat with it, once it is complete.
