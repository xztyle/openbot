### Fixed

- On a phone, a reload keeps the tab you were on (Today, Chat, Agents, Inbox or More), and the
  address names the Today, Agents and Inbox tabs. A reload on a list no longer opens the
  conversation under it.
- On a phone, a reload on a thread keeps the thread open instead of stepping back to its channel.
- More, opened from Today's header, has a back button, and Back or the edge swipe returns to Today
  instead of leaving the app.
- On a phone, Back closes an open sheet or dialog first (Jump to, the space switcher, the "+" sheet,
  row actions), instead of moving the screen under it or leaving the app.
- A link to a section of the server settings no longer freezes the app.
- An address with only a page, such as `?page=settings`, opens it over Today instead of over the
  last agent chat.
- The command bar: "Tell me…" or "Assignment…" sentences go to the lead agent instead of offering
  nothing; "ask Research Lead …" picks Research Lead, not Research; a name must end at a word end;
  "approve" or "stop" with nothing waiting no longer offers to create a #approve channel.
