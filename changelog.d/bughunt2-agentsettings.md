### Fixed

- The settings button of an agent in Fleet, its profile card and the sidebar menu opens its settings
  also for the agent whose chat you had open last. Before, nothing happened on the desktop, and on a
  phone the tab bar went away with Fleet still on the screen.
- "Forget what it learned in this chat" no longer shows the items of one agent under another. When
  you moved to another agent's settings while the list loaded, the list and its Forget button
  showed there, and the button forgot from the agent on the screen.
- Agent settings, Cost and performance: each hint sits under its own field. Before, the model
  policy hint touched the Role label under it.
- Agent settings: the Role field shows the agent's role after a Roll back or a change from another
  device. Before, it kept the old role, and a tap in and out of the field saved the old role again.
- New agent, in the Craft Studio, Modern Atelier and Riso Press themes: the dialog no longer shows
  an Agent color row. An Atelier face keeps its own colors, so a color there changed nothing.
- Agent settings, Permissions, in the web app: the Workspace only note no longer says that you can
  turn Computer Use off below. That switch is only in the desktop app, for an agent on that computer.
