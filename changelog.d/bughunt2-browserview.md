### Fixed

- A sign-in popup that a page opens from the live view in a takeover card now shows in the card, and
  the card goes back to the page when the popup closes. Before, a click on "Continue with Google" in
  the card seemed to do nothing.
- A drag in the live view that ends outside the view lets the mouse button go on the page. Before,
  the page kept the button down and selected text on every move until the next click.
- The dock over a sign-in popup of a page that an agent asked you to use shows Hand back, as over the
  page itself. Before, the popup had no Hand back until it closed.
- On a phone, a finger held still on the agent's page in the live view no longer opens the
  workspace's menu (New channel, Browse channels…) over the page.
- A phone on its side shows the browser view with the one bar of a phone, so the page has more of
  the short screen. Before, it had the tab row and the address row of a computer.
- A copy (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C) in the live view while the agent acts on the tab no longer stops the
  copies after it. Before, no copy worked again until the view reconnected.
