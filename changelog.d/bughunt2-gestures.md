### Fixed

- Phone: a long press on the message box's buttons (attach, format, send), on "Jump to latest" or on
  the space between messages no longer opens the workspace's menu. A long press on a message still
  opens its actions.
- Phone: a sheet with a handle, such as a message's actions or a profile, follows a finger that drags
  it down, and closes when you drag it far or flick it. Before, the handle did nothing.
- Image viewer: a pinch whose first finger drifted down before the second one landed no longer
  leaves the image lower and the backdrop faded.
- Phone and tablet: a text field in a message, such as a form an agent sends, selects text and offers
  Paste again. The message's touch rule, which turns off selection for the long press, reached it.
- Phone: an edge swipe while a sheet or dialog is open no longer slides the conversation away under
  it and back. The swipe goes back once nothing covers the conversation.
- Phone: holding a "Hold to confirm" button in a chat card no longer opens the message's actions
  halfway through the hold.
