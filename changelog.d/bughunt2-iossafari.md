### Fixed

- On an iPhone, turning the phone while the keyboard is open keeps the message box on the keyboard. Before, the app took the keyboard as closed: the bars over the box came back and pushed the box out of view.
- On an iPhone on its side, a form (New channel, New message and the others) keeps its field and its buttons in view while the keyboard is open. Before, the title and the buttons took all the room over the keyboard and the field was hidden under the buttons.
- On an iPhone on its side, the text and buttons of a form keep clear of the notch. In portrait with the keyboard open, a form's buttons sit on the keyboard, without the gap left for the home indicator.
- On an iPhone on its side with the keyboard open, notifications show at the top of the part of the screen over the keyboard. Before, they showed out of sight. They also keep clear of the notch.
