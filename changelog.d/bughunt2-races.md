### Fixed

- Insights: picking 30 or 90 days shows that period. Before, each pick read the period picked
  before it, and a slow answer of an older pick could replace the newer one.
- Insights: saving a goal, adding money or another change while 30 or 90 days show keeps those days.
  Before, the numbers switched to one week while the picker still said 30 or 90 days.
- Trust: an audit filter (category, outcome, agent, date) shows the rows of the filter you picked.
  Before, the list showed the rows of the filter before it, and a slow answer could replace them.
- Today: an applied or dismissed learning proposal, a resumed agent and a dismissed insight alert
  no longer come back for a minute when a read that began before your answer arrives after it.
- A reply, or the end of an agent's answer, that arrives while a thread opens shows in the thread.
  Before, it was missing until the thread changed again.
- Loops: an item that you move or decide no longer goes back to where it was when the board's
  timed read arrives after your change. The same holds for a removed Knowledge item, Focus and the
  quiet-hours settings, the agent mailboxes and the connector access links.
- Jump to keeps the conversations you visit most in their order after a reload. Before, the first
  conversation that a page opened replaced the stored visits, and a switch of server carried the
  visits of the server before.
- Today: a blocked prompt injection that you dismissed in one tab stays dismissed after a dismissal
  in another tab, and the counts of every open tab follow at once.
- A reaction's emoji stays first in the quick reactions and in Recent after a reload, and so does
  the skin tone you chose. A closed first-run hint stays closed after a reload.
