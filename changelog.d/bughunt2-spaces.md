### Fixed

- Moving the agent or channel that you have open to another space now takes you along to that space.
  Before, the conversation stayed open in the old space while its row had gone from the sidebar.
- Mark all as read in a space's menu marks only that space, its channels and its agents' chats. Before,
  it cleared every space, so the unread marks of the other spaces on the rail went too. Shift+Escape
  now also marks the agent chats of the space read, as its confirm says.
- On a phone, the space switcher has Delete space for owners and admins. Before, a space could only be
  deleted from the space rail on a computer.
- Portfolio's Rate an outcome no longer credits an agent of the space you looked at before. An agent
  picked there counts as the whole space after a switch.
- Removing an entry from Knowledge and switching space before the answer no longer shows the old
  space's knowledge in the new one.
- In the web client on a computer, Back after opening something of another space (Open on a Today card,
  Jump to, a loop decision) returns to the space you came from. Before, the step of the space you left
  was overwritten, so Back stayed in the new space.
