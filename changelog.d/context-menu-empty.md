### Fixed

- A right-click on an image, a file, a space, a channel or a sidebar row opened an empty menu, and a
  long press on a phone opened nothing. The menus show their actions again: an image offers Copy
  image, Save image, Open, Annotate and Copy link, then the message's actions. A place with no action
  of its own shows the menu around it, and a menu never opens empty.
