### Added

- A Discord channel with its own agent, or in a server with an agent, can turn on "Use the agent's
  main chat". Messages there then go to the agent's own chat with you, so it answers with all of its
  context, and its answer is posted back as a reply in the channel. Your own messages in the app,
  routines and teammates never post to Discord. A Discord message waits for its own turn instead of
  joining one that runs, and nothing joins a turn that answers Discord. A one-time routine, a wait or
  a teammate's answer that the agent starts from such a message replies there too.
