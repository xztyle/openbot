### Changed

- Show where an event check message came from as a small chip on the top edge of the reply
  bubble, with the name of the check. The centered "Event check: … triggered" row is gone. The
  first reply and the last reply of the interaction have the chip. When you write in the middle
  of the turn, or the event arrives while the agent already works, only the first reply has it.
- The chip shows the logo of the app that the event check reads, such as Slack or Linear, in place
  of the bell. The bell stays when the app has no logo or the logo does not load. The Gmail,
  Proton Mail and Discord checks now name their logo too.
