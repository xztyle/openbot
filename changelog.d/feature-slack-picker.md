### Added

- Pick Slack conversations from a list. In the Slack activity install form and in the check
  editor, load your channels, private channels, direct messages and group messages, then tick the
  ones to watch. For each one, choose all new messages or only messages that mention you. You no
  longer copy channel IDs by hand, and you can still add an ID by hand.
- Slack activity 1.3.0: the new setting "Conversations to watch" holds the choices. Existing
  settings (Watch chosen channels and Channel IDs) keep working. A direct conversation with its own
  choice is read by that choice, not as part of all direct messages.
- Event check templates can declare a setting as a picker. Older apps show such a setting as a
  plain text box.
