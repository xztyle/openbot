### Fixed

- In channels and agent chats, "Show more" no longer lies over a visible line: the text is gone
  where the button sits.
- Images and files of a message never fold: only a long text folds, and a short text with images
  shows whole.
- The result of a subtask that speaks to a person, such as "Alejandro, I redid the copy…", shows as
  a normal message, not as a folded line between agents. Agents can mark a result or a channel post
  for the people (`audience`). An open line between agents no longer repeats the first sentence of
  its text.
