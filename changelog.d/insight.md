### Added

- Insights (sidebar > More, for the owner and admins; phone: More): goals per space with progress
  over time, money per space and day (Meta Ads spend and Hotmart commissions, read each morning with
  the spaces' own helper settings and the shared keys, plus entries by hand), and the cost of each
  agent per day with its side calls and the turns that left no message. Alerts such as "spent 120
  USD in 3 days and 0 sales" also show in Needs you.
- A morning briefing in a private Briefing channel, with a notification: what moved, what waits for
  you, money, goals and the week's agent cost, written by the workspace gate model from stored data
  in one call. The time, zone, language and spaces are set in Insights > Briefing, and it can pause
  routine reports that it replaces.
- Agents see one line per goal of their space and read or move manual goals with `openbot.goals`.
- "Why is this slow?" on the working line of a channel and in an agent's DM shows where the time of
  the request went: queued, working, waiting on a teammate or on you, held by the plan limit, and
  failed attempts.
