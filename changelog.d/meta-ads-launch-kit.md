### Added

- The Meta ads helper has a launch kit that only reads. `readiness` gives one checklist of the
  token, the ad account and its payment method, the Facebook Page, the pixel events of the last 24
  hours and the daily spend cap, and says what is missing. `reach-estimate` shows Meta's audience
  and results at a few daily budgets, `targeting-search` finds interests, places and behaviors,
  `preview` saves Meta's ad previews as files to show the owner before launch, and `pixel-check`
  shows the last pixel events. Turning ads on still needs the owner's approval.
