### Added

- Manage Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads with the new `openbot-meta-ads` skill. Agents read Meta's
  results, pause losers, change budgets and bids, and create campaigns, ad sets and ads. New ads start
  paused, and a change that raises spend needs the user's approval. Every change goes to an audit log.
- Watch Meta ads without AI with three event check templates: performance limits (cost per purchase,
  ROAS, spend against the daily budget, click-through rate, no purchases), ad review problems, and new
  lead form leads.
