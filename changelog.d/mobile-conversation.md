### Changed

- On a phone, the messages of a channel, a thread and an agent DM are 16 px with more space between lines, as in Slack's app. Names, times, code and tables are larger too.
- On a phone, a tap on a face or a name opens the profile in a sheet from the bottom of the screen.
- On a phone, "Waiting for replies" and the open waits above the message box start folded to one row. A tap opens them.
- On a phone, reactions and the step trail of an agent's answer are large enough to tap, and a file card shows more of the file's name.

### Fixed

- In an agent DM on a phone, Back closes the agent's details and returns to the chat. Before, it left the DM.
- On a phone, "3 new messages since 9:05" fits the screen. Before, both ends of the pill were off the screen in some languages.
- On a touch screen, a message no longer stays highlighted after you tap it or scroll past it, and the reading rail no longer covers the right edge of the text.
- The step trail of an agent's answer shows a right arrow while the steps are closed.
- A code span that wraps keeps its frame on each line.
- The time of a note in a chat ("08:57 PM") is written as the time of a message ("8:57 PM").
