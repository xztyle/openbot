### Changed

- On a phone, the header of your DM with an agent is like Slack's: Back, the agent's face and name, the model, and More. More opens the other controls in a sheet: the apps of the chat, search, the agent's browser, the side panel and the agent's settings. A tap on the name opens the details.
- On a phone, the message box of a channel or a DM takes the width of the screen and sits just above the home indicator. The saved replies above it fade out at the edge, so you can see that the row scrolls.
- In a Safari tab on an iPhone, a swipe from the left edge of a conversation goes back to the list, as it already did in the app on the Home Screen.

### Fixed

- On an iPhone, the header of a conversation stays below the status bar, and Back can be tapped. Before, the page could scroll under the status bar after the keyboard closed, which also left an empty band under the message box.
- On a phone, Back and More in a conversation header are 44 px, the size of a finger.
- "Jump to latest" over a chat has a solid face and an edge, so the messages behind it do not show through.
