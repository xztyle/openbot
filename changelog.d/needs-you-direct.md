### Changed

- When a teammate's delegated work needs the person (a browser sign-in or step, or an approval), the
  channel that the work belongs to shows the card, Needs you opens that channel, and the notification
  on desktop, web push and the phone names the agent and the channel and opens it. The agent that
  delegated the work gets no note to pass the request on.
- Agents ask the person themselves, from any chat, when only the person can act (sign in, approve
  spending, pay or decide), instead of telling only a teammate.
