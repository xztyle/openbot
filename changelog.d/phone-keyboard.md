### Fixed

- On an iPhone, the message box sits on the keyboard while you type, and the header stays at the top of the screen. Before, the box moved to the top of the screen under the status bar, with an empty band down to the keyboard. This works in a Safari tab and in the app on the Home Screen.
- On a phone, a long draft grows the message box up to a third of the part of the screen over the keyboard, then scrolls. Before, the box could push the header and the messages out.
- On a phone, the message box of a channel takes the width of the screen, as in a DM, and the empty typing line over it is gone.
- On a phone, sheets, dialogs and notifications that open while the keyboard is open show over the keyboard, not under it.
