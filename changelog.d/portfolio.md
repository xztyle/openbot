### Added

- A Portfolio view, under More, for the owner and the admins. Value shows what each space and agent
  earns next to what it costs: the space's revenue shared by done Motion cards, weighted by priority,
  plus the outcomes that you rate, with value per dollar.
- Each agent can have a test suite: scenarios with a simulated person, a rubric and what the agent
  must never do. A run rehearses each one on the agent's own model with no tools, so nothing is sent,
  paid or written, and a judge scores it. The suite runs again 3 minutes after a change of the agent's
  instructions, model, skills or playbook, and shows which scenarios now pass or fail. A lesson that
  would change the agent is rehearsed before it applies, and the suite can warn or block it.
- About 5% of the answers to people are scored by a cheap model, with a 30-day trend per agent and a
  flag when the weekly score falls.
- Templates: export a space (agents, roles, instructions, skills, schedules, playbooks, loops and
  channels, without chats, memories, keys or customer data) and create a new space from it, or from
  the starter templates Affiliate courses, YouTube channel and Local shop. A validation stage scores
  the idea and its demand first; the agents start only when you press Start building.
- A weekly reality check per space compares what the agents said they did with evidence (links that
  answer, files that exist, sales in the money rows) and sends you a short report.
- Agents can post a card in the chat with `post_card`: a choice, approve or reject, a short form, or a
  table of options with a decision per row. You answer with taps, and the agent gets the answer as a
  message.
