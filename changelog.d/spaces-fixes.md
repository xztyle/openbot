### Fixed

- A space that you pick in the space rail stays picked. Before, it could go back to the space before it, after a save of the sidebar or when another window or tab of yours saved its own.
- Opening a conversation of another space, from search, a notification or Jump to, now shows its space. Deleting or hiding the open agent no longer moves you to another space or closes the view you are in.
- A new channel shows in its space at once, and a short connection loss no longer shows General for a moment.
