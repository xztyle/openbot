### Changed

- Your DM with an agent shows its messages as a channel does: each message has the face, the name and the time of its author, messages by one author within five minutes stand together, and there are no bubbles. On a computer, the action bar shows on hover, with reactions, Reply and More; on a phone, a long press opens the actions in a sheet. The steps of a turn fold inside the agent's message, and the day and the "New" line look as in a channel.

### Fixed

- In an agent's chat, the Delete key on the last tab of the side panel no longer stops the chat. Before, no button of the chat worked after it until you opened the chat again.
- On a phone, the search filters stand in two columns, so each one shows. Before, they were in one row that scrolled sideways, and the last filters were past the edge of the screen.
