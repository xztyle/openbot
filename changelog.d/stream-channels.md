### Added

- Agents can make and manage workspace channels from their own chat when you ask: create a channel or a group with its topic, description, instructions, visibility and members, change each member's answer mode and focus, the routing and the lead, rename, archive, restore or delete a channel, and post a brief that gives members tasks. An agent works only with channels and agents of its own space, changes only channels it is a member of or made, and leaves a short note in the channel for each change. You are always in each channel an agent makes.
