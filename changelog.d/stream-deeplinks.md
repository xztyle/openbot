### Added

- Give each place of the web client its own address, as Slack does. The address names the space and
  the channel, DM, group, thread or view that shows, for example
  `/app/market-sniper/c/<channel>/t/<thread>`. A reload, a new tab or a shared link opens the same
  place, and Back and Forward move between the places you opened. A link to a conversation of another
  space opens that space. A link to something that is gone, or that you cannot see, shows the
  activity of the space and says so.
- Add **Copy link** to the menu of a channel header. **Copy link** of a message now copies an address
  that opens the message, and marks it, in its channel or thread.
- Show the view or the thread that was open again when the desktop app starts.
