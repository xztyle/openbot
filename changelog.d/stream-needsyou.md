### Fixed

- A browser takeover now always shows its card. Before, a busy browser tab could hold the card back
  with no end, and the agent waited for an answer that nobody saw.
- A card that waits for you (a takeover, a question or an approval) now comes into view when it
  arrives in the open chat. Before, it could stay under the last message, out of sight.

### Added

- Needs you: every request of every agent that waits for you shows everywhere. The agent's DM row
  shows at once with an amber mark, also when you never wrote to it. The agent that gave it the work
  says what to do in its "Waiting for replies" list. The top bar on a computer has a Needs you button
  with the count, Activity starts with the list, and the Home of a phone has a Needs you chip. The
  badges, the tab title and the Dock count it.
- A browser takeover or a password request now sends a web push, as a question does.
- A takeover card that cannot open in this browser says where to finish the step.
