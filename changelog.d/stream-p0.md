### Fixed

- Keep the unread replies of a thread when you open its channel. Each person now has a read position
  in each thread, apart from the channel, as in Slack. A thread is read when you read it.

### Added

- Follow threads as in Slack. You follow a thread that you started, replied to or were mentioned in,
  and every thread of a DM or a group. You can stop following a thread; a later mention does not
  follow it again, your own reply does. The Threads view lists the threads that you follow.
- Mark a message unread. Your read position in the channel, or in the thread, moves to just before
  the message, so it and the messages after it are unread again.
- Search the messages of channels, groups and DMs, thread replies too. A word finds the words that
  start with it, also without accents; `"two words"` finds a phrase and `-word` leaves messages out.
  Results show only what you can see, with the matched words marked, by relevance or newest first.
