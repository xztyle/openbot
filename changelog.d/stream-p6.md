### Added

- Stop an agent that works in a workspace channel or thread. A Stop button shows next to
  "<agent> is working…", as in Slack, and stops the agent's work in that place.

### Changed

- Keep your place in a thread. A new reply moves the thread panel only when you are at the newest
  reply. When you read above it, a "3 new replies" button takes you down. The thread opens again
  where you left it, and your own reply always goes to the end.
- Show the same working line in a thread as in its channel: what the agent does now, its steps, and
  Stop.
- Open a channel where you left it when you come back to it. A channel with new messages opens at
  the first new one, as before. The bar at the top counts the new messages: "12 new messages since
  10:15".
