### Added

- Mark a message unread, in a channel or a thread, from its menu or the long-press sheet on a
  phone. The "New" line moves to the message, and the conversation stays unread until you leave it.
- Pin a message to its channel, as in Slack. A pinned message shows "Pinned by <name>", and the new
  Pins tab of the channel details lists the pins and opens each one.
- Save a message for later from its menu. A saved message shows "Saved for later".
- Follow or stop following a thread with the bell in its header or "Turn off notifications for
  replies" in the root's menu.
- Choose how a conversation notifies you with the bell in its header: your server setting, all new
  messages, just mentions or nothing, and mute. A reply notice follows the level. A DM set to just
  mentions or nothing counts only its mentions in its badge and in Activity.

### Changed

- Read threads one by one. A thread summary shows a dot while it has replies you have not read, and
  a thread opens at its first new reply with the "New" line, as a channel does.

### Fixed

- Keep a mute, a closed DM or a new order in the workspace sidebar. Before, the change could go back
  a moment later, because the host got the value from before the change.
