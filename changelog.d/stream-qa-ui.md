### Added

- ⌘J (Ctrl+J) goes to the first unread message of the conversation, as in Slack.
- A thread reply that was also sent to the channel says "Replied to a thread: …" in the channel. A
  click opens the thread with the reply marked.
- The browser tab title names the place, as Slack's: "#general - Market - OpenBot".
- Mark as read in the menu of an unread channel or DM (right-click, or a long press on a phone).
- Unreads can show all conversations, only channels or only DMs, and sort by the sidebar, newest
  or oldest.

### Changed

- On a phone, the DMs tab is the same DMs list as on a computer, with search, Unread only and a
  long press for the actions of each DM. Jump to "DMs" or "Activity", and a link to them, open
  that tab.
- On a phone, the bell of a channel opens its choices in a sheet, and a screen with Back has no
  second close button.
- A DM's menus say "Conversation details"; a DM thread names the DM without a lock; Activity shows
  a DM as "Direct message", not "in #Ana".

### Fixed

- A channel header keeps the whole channel name; the topic gives way.
- Fields in dialogs (Jump to, Browse channels, New message) and the sidebar's filter field had
  larger text than the other fields.
- On the iPhone app on the Home Screen, toasts no longer show under the status bar.
- On a phone, Search from More puts the cursor in the search field.
- In the chat with an agent, "Waiting for replies" no longer covers the saved reply chips.
