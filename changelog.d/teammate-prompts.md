### Added

- Show a teammate's question, approval or browser step in the chat of the agent that gave it the
  work, up to the agent that you talk to, on desktop and on the web. Answer it there: the answer
  goes to the teammate's request, and the card closes in both chats. A question with a private
  answer, such as a password, and a browser step show only a button that opens the teammate's chat.
  Before, the request showed only in the teammate's chat, and the teammate could wait for hours.
- Put the agent that you talk to in "Needs you" while its teammate waits for you. The notification
  of the request names both agents and opens the chat of the agent that you talk to, also when the
  teammate's notifications are off, as they are for a background teammate.
- Tell the agent that you talk to at once, with one short note, when its teammate starts to wait
  for you. The agent then says so first in its next reply to you.
