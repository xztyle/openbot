### Fixed

- Show each message of a channel, thread or DM below the one before it, always. Before, a message
  could be cut off and the next message, or its day divider, was drawn over it.
- Open a link to an old channel message after more than one older page loads. Before, the channel
  stopped after the first page and stayed at the newest messages.
- Stream a reply into a long agent DM faster: a delta draws only the message that changed.
