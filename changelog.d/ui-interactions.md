### Changed

- A right-click or a long press on a message now offers what it landed on first: an image offers Copy image, Save image, Open and Annotate; a file Open and Download; a link Open and Copy link; a code block Copy code; selected text Copy and Quote in reply; the author's face View profile and Direct message. The message's own actions follow under a divider. Shift+F10 opens the menu from the keyboard.
- Focus rings that drew nothing on sidebar rows, tabs and profile triggers now show. The options of a question get a pointer and a pressed state; every button darkens while held; static rows no longer light on hover. Tooltips of Send, New message, Details and the shortcuts help show their keys. Every menu puts its destructive action last, in red, after a separator.
