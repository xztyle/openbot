### Changed

- Every dialog, sheet, confirmation and the image viewer now dims and blurs the page under it the
  same way, keeps clear of a phone's notch and home indicator, and fades out when it closes. Before,
  some opened over a nearly clear page and others over an almost solid one.
- The image viewer has Copy, which puts the image on the clipboard, and Annotate, which opens the
  image editor when one is set up. On a phone, a drag down also closes a form sheet.
