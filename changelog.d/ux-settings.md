### Changed

- On a phone, Settings and Server settings work like iOS Settings and Slack's app: a list of sections first, and a tap opens a section with Back to the list.
- On a phone, settings, the agent's settings, dialogs, pickers, Secrets, Usage, Schedule and Tasks use larger text (16 px) and 44 px controls and switches, so they are easy to read and to tap.
- "Add secret" opens the same dialog as the other create flows (a full-height sheet on a phone), with the error under the field.

### Fixed

- On an iPhone, Safari no longer zooms in when you tap a text box in a dialog or in the agent's settings (for example, "What should this agent help with?" or the instructions).
- On a phone, the invite email box of Server settings stays inside its card, and the last setting can scroll clear of the "Server settings unavailable" notice.
- The Usage, Schedule and Tasks pages no longer draw a focus ring around their title when they open.
- A tap on a button in the agent's settings panel no longer leaves a hover colour on a touch screen.
