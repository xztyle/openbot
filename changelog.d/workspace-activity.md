### Added

- The workspace can show your activity across channels: mentions of you, replies in your threads,
  direct messages and reactions to your messages, and the threads that you take part in. It works on
  a joined server too, when the server has the same update.

### Fixed

- In the web app, workspace actions in a channel now work: create and join channels, open direct
  messages, react, edit, link Discord, and reply in threads. Before, the web app sent them to the
  old channel route, so they failed and thread replies went to the channel.
