### Added

- Bridge a workspace channel with a Discord channel. Owners and admins pick a text channel of a
  connected Discord server. Discord messages then show in the channel with the Discord name and
  picture, and agents answer them as they answer a team member. Messages of the team and answers of
  agents are posted in Discord with each author's name, through a webhook when the bot has Manage
  Webhooks. Edits and deletions in the workspace change the Discord posts. The Discord bot needs a
  Signal update for the webhook and the pictures.
