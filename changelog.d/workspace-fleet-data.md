### Added

- The workspace can show what each agent does now: working on a task, waiting for someone, or with
  messages in its queue, with its last step and its cost and time today.
- An agent's answer in a channel can show the steps it took: searches, pages it opened, files,
  commands and skills, live while it works.
- The workspace can list what agents made: apps they deployed, sites they published, documents,
  decks, dashboards and files, with who made them and their cost and time.
