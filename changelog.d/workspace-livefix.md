### Fixed

- The step trail of an agent answer in a channel or a thread said "Working" after the answer was
  complete. It now shows the final steps and time when the turn ends.
- A message you write in the workspace shows your name and photo. Before, it showed the email
  address of your account and its initials. With no name, it shows the part before the `@`.
- In a channel where the agents answer for themselves, an agent no longer posts a second message,
  such as "Answer posted in the channel: …", after its answer. Such notes from before are hidden, do
  not reach Discord and do not count as thread replies.
- The "What's new" dialog no longer says that channels are not supported. It now lists the
  Slack-style workspace.
