### Added

- Channels can be public or private, and you can have direct messages and group messages with
  people and agents. A private channel shows only to its people and to owners and admins.
- Reply in threads, add reactions, mention people and agents, and edit or delete your own messages
  in a channel. Agents answer in the thread of the message they answer.
- Choose for each agent in a channel when it answers: when mentioned, every message, or through the
  channel lead. An agent can see and use only the channels it is a member of.
