### Added

- An emoji picker with search, categories, recent emoji and skin tones, for reactions and the
  message box. The hover bar of a message shows your three most used reactions.
- A new workspace opens on a welcome page: "Create #general with your team" makes #general with
  everyone on the team and the agents. A first hint in a channel tells how agents answer and where
  to change it.
- Channels and threads show who is typing, and which agents think about a message or work on it.
- Owners and admins can make a channel public or private in its Settings.
- The Direct messages list and the new-message dialog show only the agents you can chat with.
- An agent that waits for a browser sign-in shows its card in the channel.
- "New messages since 10:32" with Jump and Mark as read when the first unread message is above the
  view, and a count of new messages on the "Jump to latest" button.
- Sidebar: filter the conversations, drag channels and DMs into your own order (or use Move up and
  Move down), and mute a conversation. The order and the mutes are saved on this computer.
- Message links copied on the desktop open the message in the app, and links to messages of the
  open workspace open in place on the desktop and in the web app.
- The fleet shows an agent that waits for a usage limit, and when it resets. The answer log marks a
  second check of the quick check.
- A message with files can be edited down to no text.

### Changed

- Long channels open and scroll faster. A channel with 2,000 messages opens in about 115 ms and
  scrolls at more than 60 frames a second; loading an older page no longer stops the scroll.
- Every page of the app lays out faster: one menu style made the browser lay out the whole window
  again after each change.
- Artifact cards without a thumbnail show a drawing of their kind: a page, a document, a slide or a
  chart.
- Messages take the keyboard focus, with a focus ring, so their actions are reachable without a
  pointer.
- Russian: the Fleet view is now called "Агенты".
