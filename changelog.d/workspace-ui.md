### Added

- OpenBot opens as a Slack-style workspace on the desktop and in the web app. The sidebar has
  Threads, Activity, Fleet, Artifacts, your channels and your direct messages, with one row for each
  agent. For the owner of a server, an agent's DM is its main chat, with its steps and reasoning.
- Channels and DMs show messages as Slack rows: grouped by author, with day and "new" dividers,
  reactions, thread summaries, edits and deletes, mentions of you, the steps of each agent answer,
  and cards for the apps, sites and files that agents share. "Thinking" and "working" lines show
  which agents prepare an answer. Long channels stay smooth, load older messages as you scroll up,
  and new messages arrive without a reload.
- The message box mentions people, agents, `@channel` and `@here`, links channels with `#`, has a
  formatting toolbar and the usual key chords, keeps a draft for each conversation, and edits your
  last message with Up.
- Threads open in a panel at the right, with "Also send to #channel". Channel details show the
  topic and description, the members with the answer mode and focus note of each agent, why each
  agent answered or stayed quiet (owners and admins), the Discord bridge, and archive and leave.
- Create channels, start DMs and group DMs, browse and join public channels, and jump to any
  conversation with ⌘K or Ctrl+K. Alt+Up and Alt+Down go to the previous and next conversation, and
  Escape closes the panel or marks the channel read.
- On a phone the web app shows one pane at a time: the list, the conversation, then the thread or
  the details, each with a back button.
- The workspace menu keeps the rest of OpenBot in reach: search, new agent, Marketplace, Routines,
  server settings, and the classic agent sidebar.
